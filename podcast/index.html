Why I started

From anxiety to understanding.

Like many people, I feel the uncertainty around AI: jobs changing, new opportunities appearing, and a constant stream of confident, conflicting claims online. Social media can leave us with misconceptions long before we have the context to question them.

I started these interviews to work through that uncertainty myself. I want to understand how people who study and build AI see its development, challenge my assumptions, and develop my own framework and philosophy for thinking about it.

I'm sharing the conversations so others can do the same. Each guest brings a perspective to consider, and each interview is a chance to ask a better question.

Questions I keep coming back to

Technology. Work. Being human.

01

What is changing about work?

Which tasks will AI take on, what new roles might emerge, and what should students spend their time learning?

02

What should we believe?

How do we separate real progress from hype, recognize uncertainty, and form judgments beyond a social media headline?

03

What stays human?

What happens to our relationships, curiosity, and independent thinking as AI becomes part of everyday life?
