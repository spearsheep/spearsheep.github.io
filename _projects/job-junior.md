## What I built

I developed an end-to-end outbound automation system that used Playwright, LangChain, FAISS, and QLoRA to extract job descriptions, match profiles, and draft targeted outreach. Across more than 1,000 targets, the system achieved a response rate above **15%**.

An asyncio-based ETL pipeline increased throughput by **6×** and supported daily collection from more than 800 companies. The product secured a **$2K+/month** pilot with five recruiting firms while keeping the longer-term vision focused on a one-stop application experience for students.
