Anima Brain in action

Three short English demos of character speech, live audio calls, and animated video calls.

One brain. A voice, a conversation, a face.

Three short clips from the Anima Brain Python SDK and local showcase. Press play to hear each example. These are configured-provider demonstrations with fictional exchanges.

02 / LIVE AUDIO CALL · 5.9 SECONDS

Just call. Just talk.

Spoken input passes through recognition, the brain, and character speech. The audio is the actual call response; the interface was re-recorded around it.

Exchange transcript

User: “I was going to bed an hour ago. Somehow I'm still scrolling. Don't judge me.”

Rina: “I wouldn't dare judge. I'm just impressed by your commitment to procrastination.”

03 / ANIMATED VIDEO CALL · 6.6 SECONDS

A face in the conversation

The S2 avatar and speech were captured together over RTC, then trimmed together to preserve synchronization.

Exchange transcript

User: “Pizza, or should I pretend I'm actually going to cook?”

Rina: “Do you want the truth or a thinly veiled excuse to eat pizza? Because I can deliver both.”

Try it yourself

Anima Brain on GitHub includes the SDK, provider configuration, and showcase setup. For the ready-to-use consumer app, visit Anima Lab.

The clips are 4K presentations with English captions and no background music. The avatar stream itself is 768 × 1364, preserved inside the composition. These short excerpts are not latency benchmarks. See recording provenance and media terms.
