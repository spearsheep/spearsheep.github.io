Anima Lab

A real-time, multimodal AI companion built across web, iOS, and Android.

I founded Anima Lab to explore what an AI companion feels like when memory, emotion, voice, images, and tools operate as one coherent system.

RoleFounder & Founding Engineer
SurfacesWeb · native iOS · Android
StackNext.js · SwiftUI · React Native · Convex

What I built

I took the product from zero to launch on three clients sharing a TypeScript and Convex backend. The agent combines photorealistic image generation, voice synthesis, tool calling, and a self-evolving memory system that weighs relevance, recency, and importance before consolidating or pruning memories.

The system increased memory recall accuracy by 13% and monthly active use by 20%. I also led a team of more than five people and built the acquisition infrastructure that improved signup conversion by 15%.
